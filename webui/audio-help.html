The Audio page belongs to the StratuxAudio add-on: an AirPlay receiver that plays music from an iPhone through a USB sound dongle into the audio panel.

Now Playing shows the song, artist, album and cover art the phone is sending, and the phone's volume setting.

Audio Status checks the AirPlay receiver, the USB sound dongle and the output setting. "Sending audio" means sound is reaching the dongle right now.

Output level sets how loud the dongle drives the audio panel. Set it once, with the phone at full volume, to the loudest level that doesn't distort; after that, use the phone's volume. It's saved on the Stratux and comes back after a restart.

Sound tunes the music for your audio panel and headset:

  • Mono mixes both channels into one. Use it if your panel's music input is mono (most are). Switching restarts AirPlay for about 2 seconds.
  • Equalizer: seven bands from 60 Hz to 15 kHz, ±12 dB, or a preset. Rich is the bass-and-treble lift most headphones add over Bluetooth; Cockpit trims engine rumble and lifts voices and detail. Changes apply live while music plays. When you boost, the overall level drops a little so nothing distorts.
  • Loudness adds bass and treble as you turn the phone down.

Everything is saved on the Stratux and comes back after a restart.

To play: on the iPhone, open Control Center or the music app's AirPlay button and choose the speaker name shown here.

The AirPlay receiver runs at low priority, so traffic and weather always come first.
